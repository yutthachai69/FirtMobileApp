# RelayContent Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026

RelayContent helps creators organize media and publish content to TikTok. This
policy explains the information processed when you use the service.

## Information we process

- Account details such as display name and email address.
- Authentication and TikTok connection tokens, stored by the service in
  encrypted form and used only for authorized actions.
- Media files, captions, product selections, schedules, and publishing status
  that you choose to submit.
- Device and push-notification tokens when notification support is enabled.
- Basic diagnostic information needed to secure and operate the service.

## How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, import and store requested media,
prepare and publish content, show job status, send requested notifications,
prevent abuse, and maintain service reliability.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share information with TikTok or other
providers only when needed to deliver an action you request, such as connecting
your TikTok account or publishing a selected video. Service providers may
process data on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.

## Storage and retention

Data is retained while your account or an active workflow requires it. You can
disconnect TikTok and request account/data deletion. Backups and security logs
may persist for a limited period where required for recovery, fraud prevention,
or legal compliance.

## Your choices

You can review or remove connected accounts, stop notifications, and request
access, correction, or deletion of your information.

## Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the project owner through
the [RelayContent project repository](https://github.com/yutthachai69/FirtMobileApp).

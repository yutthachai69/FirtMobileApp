## Brand & Style
This design system is tailored specifically for high-velocity Thai TikTok Shop affiliate creators, influencers, and digital commerce entrepreneurs. The atmosphere is energetic, focused, and unmistakably modern—combining the electric pulse of live commerce with the calm precision of a pro-grade workflow engine.

### Design Persona & Core Values
- **Creator-Centric & Decisive**: The interface exists to convert affiliate data, brand briefs, and video deadlines into straightforward actions. Visual clutter is eliminated to reduce cognitive load during fast content creation sprints.
- **Thai-First Localization**: Crafted natively for Thai creators. Microcopy employs natural, respectful, and energetic Thai phrasing (e.g., หน้าหลัก, สินค้า, สร้าง, คอนเทนต์, โปรไฟล์), completely eliminating English telemetry and robotic translations.
- **Electric Slate Aesthetic**: High-contrast, premium dark mode featuring refined deep slate backdrops paired with neon cyan/mint velocity highlights and warm coral revenue/urgency indicators.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Modern Dark Surface Tiering** and **Subtle Glass Neon Accents**:
- Deep, desaturated slate backgrounds avoid true black (#000000) to minimize eye strain during nocturnal editing sessions while maintaining OLED battery efficiency.
- Luminescent electric cyan acts as the core driver for creation, analytics gains, and positive forward momentum.
- Electric coral/orange provides instant peripheral awareness for commission earnings, urgent video alerts, and expiring product samples.

## Colors
The color palette establishes immediate visual priority in a nocturnal creative environment. Ambient slate surfaces create depth, vibrant mint-cyan guides execution, and warm coral indicates monetary and temporal value.

### Color Archetypes & Tokens
- **Background Base (`#0F1117`)**: The global viewport canvas; low visual vibration, calibrated for continuous mobile usage.
- **Surface Level 1 (`#161922`)**: Neutral background for structural containers, bottom navigation, and top bars.
- **Surface Level 2 (`#1E2230`)**: Elevated card containers, modal sheets, and interactive list items.
- **Surface Level 3 (`#272C3D`)**: Highlighted states, active chips, and nested sub-cards.
- **Primary Accent (`#00F2FE` - Electric Mint/Cyan)**: Primary actions, creation flows, progress milestones, and active tab highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#FF4565` - Coral Flame)**: Hot affiliate deals, commission percentage badges, critical deadlines (เช่น ต้องทำ, หลุดจอง), and primary video recording prompts.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#2EE59D` - Affiliate Green)**: Positive monetary revenue, confirmed payout tags, and verified affiliate sync states.
- **Text Primary (`#F8FAFC`)**: High-contrast primary copy and numeric figures.
- **Text Secondary (`#94A3B8`)**: Subtext, inactive labels, and secondary metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#64748B`)**: Timestamps, placeholder labels, and disabled states.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#262B3C`)**: Crisp structural lines without high friction.

## Typography
Typography is optimized specifically for loopless Thai character legibility combined with crisp Western numerals for live revenue and sales tracking.

### Type Pairing & Hierarchy
- **Display & Numerical Headings (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric structure and immediate clarity for currency figures, affiliate commission percentages, and video counts.
- **Thai Body & Microcopy (`Noto Sans Thai / Noto Sans`)**: Ensures zero overlapping vowel marks (`วรรณยุกต์`) and clean modern line rhythm even at 10px and 12px status pill sizes.

### Usage Standards
- Numbers with currency (฿) must consistently use tabular or proportional figures with negative letter spacing for immediate dashboard scanning.
- Minimum line height for Thai body text is pinned to 1.5x font size to accommodate ascenders and descenders seamlessly.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres strictly to an iPhone viewport target of **390 × 844 px** (CSS points), built using an ergonomic thumb-zone framework.

### Viewport & Grid Architecture
- **Global Safe Padding**: 16px (`screen-edge`) horizontal margin across all standard screens.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Base 4px/8px modular grid. Spacing between major content sections is set to 20px (`space-lg`), while micro-item gaps within cards sit at 8px (`space-xs`).
- **Touch Ergonomics**: All interactive elements (CTA buttons, tab bar items, close triggers, status selectors) enforce a strict minimum boundary of 44 × 44 pt (`touch-min`).
- **Bottom Clearance**: 84px reserved for the persistent bottom navigation bar + 34px iOS Home Indicator safe area offset. No actionable button may render within 96px of the bottom screen edge without floating clearance.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids artificial skeuomorphism, leaning instead into controlled tonal layer stacking and localized neon light refraction.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Base Canvas - `#0F1117`)**: Base screen ground plane.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Navigation - `#161922`)**: Top bars and bottom navigation with subtle top border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` and 16px background blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
3. **Level 2 (Standard Cards - `#1E2230`)**: Elevated content containers. Surfaced with a 1px boundary stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` and a subtle directional shadow `0 4px 12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.35)`.
4. **Level 3 (Interactive Promos & Center CTA)**: Features an energetic cyan ambient bleed: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.22)`.
5. **Level 4 (Modals & Action Sheets)**: Sheet overlay backed by `rgba(8, 10, 15, 0.75)` dark blur scrim. The sheet surface `#1E2230` casts `0 -8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

## Shapes
A unified rounded geometry balances handheld device ergonomics with a tactile, content-ready appearance.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base Components (`8px / 0.5rem`)**: Status tags, commission pills, and nested thumbnails.
- **Interactive Controls & Inputs (`12px / 0.75rem`)**: Text fields, filter chips, secondary actions, and list items.
- **Content Cards & Containers (`16px / 1rem`)**: Product cards, workflow status cards, and notification banners.
- **Overlays & Bottom Sheets (`24px / 1.5rem` top radii)**: Creator action sheets and draft detail panels.
- **Floating Controls & Icon Bubbles (`9999px / Full Pill`)**: Center "สร้าง" bottom tab trigger, floating action badges, and quick filter indicators.

## Components

### 1. Bottom Navigation Bar (แถบเมนูด้านล่าง)
- **Viewport Structure**: Fixed 390px width, height 64px + safe area padding. Background `#161922` at 90% opacity with 20px blur.
- **The 5 Tabs**:
  1. `หน้าหลัก` (Home icon)
  2. `สินค้า` (Shopping bag with commission dot)
  3. `สร้าง` (Elevated center button: 48x48px circle, vibrant gradient from `#00F2FE` to `#2EE59D` with dark slate glyph, raised -12px above bar)
  4. `คอนเทนต์` (Video grid icon with count indicator)
  5. `โปรไฟล์` (Avatar bubble with verified badge)
- **States**: Inactive tabs render in `#94A3B8` (10px label); active tab illuminates in `#00F2FE` with a 3px active dot indicator below.

### 2. Affiliate Product Card (การ์ดสินค้าคอมมิชชัน)
- **Structure**: Horizontal layout (Surface Level 2), 80x80px square image container on left with rounded corners (8px).
- **Commission Highlight**: Dedicated pill tag at top right of image containing `คอมมิชชัน 18%` in secondary coral flame (`#FF4565` text on `rgba(255, 69, 101, 0.12)` background).
- **Price & Revenue**: Bold Thai Baht display (`฿299`) paired with expected net earnings (`รับ ฿53.82/ชิ้น` in `#2EE59D`).
- **Action Triggers**: One-tap `ขอตัวอย่างฟรี` (Request sample) or `เพิ่มลงคลิป` (Add to video) button with minimum 44px hit bounds.

### 3. Content Pipeline Status Cards (การ์ดสถานะคอนเทนต์)
Color-coded pipeline tags aligned directly with Thai affiliate workflows:
- **ต้องทำ (To-Do)**: `#FF4565` (Coral) tag with alert icon. Used for urgent sample-received videos nearing the 7-day posting deadline.
- **กำลังสร้าง (In Progress)**: `#FBBF24` (Amber) tag with script/camera icon.
- **รอตรวจ (Under Review)**: `#38BDF8` (Sky) tag for brand approval checkpoints.
- **ตั้งเวลาไว้ (Scheduled)**: `#A78BFA` (Purple) tag with clock display.
- **โพสต์แล้ววันนี้ (Posted Today)**: `#2EE59D` (Mint) tag with live play-count link.

### 4. Actionable Alerts (แถบแจ้งเตือนด่วน)
- Compact banner with 12px border radius, anchored below the top bar.
- Background: `rgba(0, 242, 254, 0.08)` with solid left border accent (3px `#00F2FE`).
- Microcopy Example: *"สินค้า ‘เซรั่มใบบัวบก’ กำลังแมสใน TikTok ตอนนี้! ยอดสั่งซื้อเพิ่มขึ้น 142% วันนี้"* with clear text CTA link *"หยิบใส่ตะกร้า >"*.

### 5. Quick Create Bottom Sheet (แผ่นสร้างสรรค์ด่วน)
- Slides upward with 24px top rounded corners from the bottom navigation.
- Provides immediate actions: `ถ่ายคลิปรีวิวสินค้า`, `ใช้ไอเดียสคริปต์ AI`, and `นำเข้าคลิปจากมือถือ`.
- Every action item maintains minimum 54px vertical height for effortless thumb engagement.
